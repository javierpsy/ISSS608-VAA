This presentation summarizes the exploratory visual analytics performed on a motor insurance portfolio dataset using the IDEA framework. The analysis focuses on portfolio composition, claim behaviour, underwriting profitability, and emerging risk patterns that may influence portfolio sustainability and pricing adequacy.

Objectives

  • Evaluate the composition and risk distribution of the motor insurance portfolio

  • Analyse claim behaviour and underwriting profitability patterns

  • Identify high-risk customer and vehicle segments associated with higher claim exposure

  • Support data-driven pricing, underwriting, and portfolio management decisions

This study applies the IDEA framework to evaluate the motor insurance portfolio from both risk and profitability perspectives. The analysis focuses on identifying claim behaviour patterns, underwriting inefficiencies, and high-risk policy segments that may impact portfolio sustainability. Through exploratory visual analytics, the study aims to support more informed pricing strategies, portfolio optimisation, and risk management decisions. The IDEA framework provides a structured approach for transforming raw insurance data into actionable business insights for management decision-making.

Portfolio Overview

Dataset Summary

  • Years Covered: 2022–2024

  • Total Policy Records: 354,140

  • Policy-level motor insurance portfolio

  • Customer, vehicle, claims, and financial variables analysed

Key Areas of Analysis

  • Claim behaviour patterns

  • Underwriting profitability

  • High-risk customer segments

  • Vehicle risk exposure

  • Pricing adequacy evaluation

This slide provides a high-level overview of the motor insurance portfolio dataset used in the analysis. The dataset contains more than 350,000 policy-level records collected between 2022 and 2024, covering customer, vehicle, claims, and financial-related variables.

The analysis focuses on understanding portfolio composition, claim behaviour, underwriting profitability, and identifying high-risk customer segments. Particular attention is given to pricing adequacy and vehicle-related risk exposure, which are important factors affecting insurance profitability and portfolio sustainability.

Portfolio Composition Analysis

Claim Status Composition

  • 85.7% of policies did not report claims

  • Claim activity is concentrated within a smaller segment of policyholders

  • A minority of policies contributes disproportionately to portfolio risk exposure

 

Premium Distribution

  • Premium values are highly right-skewed across the portfolio

  • Most policies fall within lower-to-moderate premium ranges

  • A smaller group of high-premium policies contributes substantially to portfolio revenue

This slide provides an overview of the portfolio structure and overall claim concentration. The claim status distribution shows that most policies remain claim-free, while claims are concentrated within a smaller proportion of policyholders. The premium distribution further highlights strong right-skewness, where most policies are concentrated within lower-to-moderate premium ranges alongside a smaller segment of high-premium policies. These findings indicate varying customer risk profiles and provide context for the subsequent analysis on profitability, underwriting performance, and high-risk portfolio segments.

Claim Behaviour Analysis

Yearly Portfolio Performance

  • Portfolio risk indicators increased steadily from 2022 to 2024

  • Loss ratio rose significantly, indicating growing profitability pressure

  • Claim frequency and underpricing jointly contributed to underwriting deterioration

 

Claim Severity Distribution

  • Higher vehicle value segments exhibit wider claim severity distributions

  • Tail-risk exposure is more pronounced among higher-value vehicles

  • Variability in incurred losses suggests uneven financial risk concentration

This slide examines claim behaviour and loss exposure within the motor insurance portfolio. The yearly portfolio performance chart shows that claim rate, loss ratio, and underpricing risk increased steadily from 2022 to 2024, suggesting worsening underwriting conditions over time. The claim severity chart further shows that higher-value vehicle segments exhibit wider incurred-loss distributions and stronger tail-risk behaviour. Together, these findings suggest that portfolio profitability pressure is driven by both rising claim trends over time and uneven claim severity across vehicle value segments.

Policyholder Risk Profile Analysis

Driver Age Composition

  • Portfolio exposure is concentrated among middle-aged drivers

  • Drivers aged 40–59 form more than half of insured policyholders

  • Younger drivers represent a smaller share of the portfolio

 

Driving Experience Profile

  • Highly experienced drivers show the widest experience range

  • New drivers have a more uniform and narrow driving profile

  • Experience alone may not fully explain claim volatility

This slide summarises the policyholder risk profile based on age and driving experience. The age composition chart shows that portfolio exposure is concentrated among middle-aged drivers, especially those aged 40 to 59, while younger drivers form a relatively small share of the portfolio. The driving experience chart further indicates that highly experienced drivers have wider variability in driving tenure, while new drivers show a more consistent profile. These findings suggest that although the portfolio is largely made up of mature and experienced drivers, customer risk should still be assessed together with claim behaviour, vehicle characteristics, and policy type.
